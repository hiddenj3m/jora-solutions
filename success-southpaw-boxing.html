Client context

A serious local gym needed a site that made training feel clear and easy to join.

Southpaw Boxing & Fitness Gym offers fighters classes, junior box fit, public box fit, mixed boxing, and one-to-one coaching in West Bromwich. The website needed to communicate standards, coaches, class times, location, and next steps without making interested members hunt for information.

What Jora focused on

  • Built the website from the ground up around local search and bookings.
  • Created landing sections for classes, coaching, philosophy, and location.
  • Structured content around West Bromwich boxing gym searches.
  • Made enquiry routes clear for parents, beginners, adults, and one-to-one coaching.
