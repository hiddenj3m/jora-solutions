Client context

A boxing equipment brand needed product pages that helped customers choose.

AC Fight Gear sells boxing equipment including gloves, pads, and mouth guards. The website needed to explain training use cases, glove selection, durability, returns, delivery, reviews, and product benefits in a way that could support both search visibility and purchase confidence.

What Jora focused on

  • Built the Shopify site from the ground up.
  • Created SEO-friendly product and landing page structure.
  • Added buyer guidance around glove type, weight, use case, and training level.
  • Connected product positioning with conversion-focused calls to action.
