Client context

A food brand needed search content that matched how people cook, shop, and buy in bulk.

Ever East has a strong story, a focused product range, and a clear offer around complete dry marinades. The site needed more structured metadata and content so product pages, recipes, blog posts, and wholesale messaging could support different customer needs without relying on a small set of pages.

What Jora focused on

  • Optimised metadata so pages explain their value more clearly in search.
  • Built content around recipes and product use cases.
  • Improved relevance for retail customers and wholesale buyers.
  • Gave the site more topical depth around marinades, cooking, and flavour.
