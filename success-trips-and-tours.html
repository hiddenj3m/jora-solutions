Client context

Ranking for your own name isn't the same as being found.

Before the programme, almost all of Trips & Tours' search traffic came from people who already knew the brand. That's a sign a site is invisible to the much larger group of people who are searching for a destination, trip type, or travel question, not a company name. The opportunity was to build genuine discovery, not just protect brand search.

What Jora focused on

  • Mapping destination, trip-type, and question-led search intent.
  • New and restructured pages built around non-brand searches.
  • Technical SEO and internal linking to support the new pages.
  • Monthly tracking that separates brand from non-brand performance.
