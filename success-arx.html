Client context

Workwear and PPE buyers need to trust specification before they trust the brand.

ARX sells into a category where the wrong product can mean a safety failure, so search visibility and product pages both need to work harder than in a typical retail category. Jora's job is to keep ARX visible for the right specification-led searches and keep the ecommerce experience clear enough to convert that trust into a sale.

What Jora focused on

  • Ongoing technical and content SEO across the product catalogue.
  • Ecommerce optimisation on category and product pages.
  • Monthly reporting so performance stays visible, not assumed.
  • Prioritising the next highest-value SEO and CRO opportunity each month.
