Client context

A premium garment-care brand needed more than one page to explain its value.

A one-page site could introduce the offer, but it could not fully capture the different reasons people search before trusting a specialist with valuable clothing.

What Jora focused on

  • Expanded the page structure around valuable customer searches.
  • Made the service proposition clearer for high-value garment care.
  • Created room for FAQs, process content, trust proof, and blog-led discovery.
  • Kept the experience focused on bookings and enquiries.
