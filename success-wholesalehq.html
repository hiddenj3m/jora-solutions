Client context

A broad wholesale catalogue needs structure, not scattered product pages.

WholesaleHQ has a wide product range and a strong operational offer around no minimum order quantity, seasonal gifting, product categories, custom packaging, and wholesale support. The SEO challenge is making the right pages exist for the right buying questions, then keeping the site fresh enough to cover new retail and wholesale opportunities.

What Jora focused on

  • Full-site optimisation across category, collection, support, and content pages.
  • New weekly pages targeting retail and wholesale topics.
  • Search-led page planning for product categories and buyer education.
  • LinkedIn content support to strengthen brand activity beyond the website.
