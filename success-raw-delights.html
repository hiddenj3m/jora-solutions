Client context

A large catalogue and a physical retail operation raised the stakes on migration.

Raw Delights needed a commerce platform that could carry a large, detailed product catalogue without losing accuracy, while still supporting the operational reality of a business trading across retail and online. A rushed migration risked broken product data, lost collections, and a storefront that could not scale with the catalogue.

What Jora focused on

  • Full catalogue mapping before any migration work began.
  • A master catalogue structure to keep product data consistent.
  • Shopify architecture built around collections and future growth.
  • Operational ecommerce setup that reflects how the business actually trades.
