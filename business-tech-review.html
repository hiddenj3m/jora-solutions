Who it's for

Businesses dealing with disconnected software, repetitive admin, or a change they want to get right.

Disconnected software

Different tools that don't talk to each other, so information gets copied by hand.

Repetitive administration

The same manual steps every week, with no time to fix the process behind them.

A planned change

A new location, system, or process you want to set up properly the first time.

Examples to bring

You don't need to diagnose it yourself — just describe it.

  • “We copy orders between systems.”
  • “Nobody knows which quotes need chasing.”
  • “We need to set up a new location properly.”

What the review covers

A clear next step, not unlimited consulting.

Included

  • A discussion of one issue and its context
  • A short written summary of what we covered
  • A recommended next step
  • An indicative cost range, where there's enough information

Not included

  • Live repairs or fixes during the call
  • A full security audit
  • Detailed technical architecture
  • Migration planning or unrestricted access to your systems

What happens afterwards

One of three honest outcomes.

A direct quote

For work that's straightforward and well understood after the review.

Paid discovery

For work that needs more investigation before it can be properly scoped and priced.

Honest advice

Sometimes the answer is that no project is needed yet — we'll tell you that too.
