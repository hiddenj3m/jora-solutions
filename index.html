Before you get in touch

Questions people ask before getting started.

We already have an IT provider — can you still help?

Yes. Most of our work is scoped around a specific project or problem, not an ongoing contract that replaces what you already have in place.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. We agree what's included and the price before any paid work starts — nothing is charged for finding out what you need.

Is support remote or on-site?

Most issues are resolved remotely. On-site visits are arranged where the problem genuinely needs it, within our service area.

What hours does support cover?

Coverage and response times are confirmed when you submit a request — we don't advertise round-the-clock cover we can't actually staff.

What happens after a project is implemented?

You get a handover and documentation. Ongoing support beyond that is a separate, clearly agreed service — not an automatic add-on.
