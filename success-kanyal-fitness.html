Client context

A coaching offer with specialist value needed search visibility to match.

Kanyal Fitness offers online metabolic health and fitness coaching, with services around weight loss, type 2 diabetes support, metabolic syndrome, GP referrals, lifestyle coaching, injury rehabilitation, and strength and conditioning. The site needed more structured content so people searching for specific health and fitness problems could find a relevant path into the offer.

What Jora focused on

  • Moved the site beyond no meaningful search visibility.
  • Targeted more search queries and specialist terms.
  • Created blogs for professionals with specific health concerns.
  • Aligned content around coaching services and practical buyer questions.
